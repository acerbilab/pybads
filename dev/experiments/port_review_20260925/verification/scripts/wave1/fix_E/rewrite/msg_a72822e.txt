fix: a failed GP fit raises the noise's lower bound by noise_nudge[1] per failure, as MATLAB BADS (W1-12)

After each failed fit, _robust_gp_fit_ read the lower bound of the noise
from tmp_gp, which already held the earlier raises, and raised it by the
cumulative nudge of the start: after k failures the bound stood at
lb0 + k(k+1)/2 (at the default noise_nudge [1, 0]), the fifth failure put it
above the upper bound 5 and set_bounds raised ValueError, and noise_nudge[1]
was never read. A one-element nudge was completed into a 2x1 array. With
use_slice_sampler=True, the bound overtook the start, and the third
consecutive failure stopped the run with SliceSampler's "The initial
starting point X0 is outside the bounds" (the B5 verifier's N1).

MATLAB raises the start of the noise by the cumulative nudge(1) and its
lower bound by nudge(2) per failure, which is 0 at the default NoiseNudge
[1 0], and completes a one-element nudge with half of it for the bound
(gpHyperOptimize.m:6-7, 160-166). The bound now comes from the bound at
entry, which the GP given holds, raised by noise_nudge[1] per failure, so
at the default it stays in place; the start keeps its cumulative nudge; a
one-element noise_nudge, or a number, is completed as MATLAB completes it.

This moves default deterministic runs, whose refits often fail once or
twice (B5 verifier: 13 of the 21 refits with a failure ended with the noise
at the nudged bound). Tests: test_robust_fit_noise_nudge (the bound and the
start of the noise at each of seven tries, at the default nudge and at a
one-element one) and test_robust_fit_slice_sampler_starts_within_bounds
(four failures with the slice sampler). Fingerprint 3a1c15091c801614
(bfbc6d6737e99d88 at the parent).
