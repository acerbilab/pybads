fix: the calibration test of fewer than three GP statistics uses the chi-square quantiles, which were halved (W1-5)

With fewer than three statistics of the GP prediction, _is_gp_refit_time_ tests their sum of squares against the quantiles alpha/2 and 1 - alpha/2 of the chi-square distribution with n degrees of freedom, but computed them as gammaincinv(n / 2, p), half the quantiles: at the default alpha = 1e-6 the bounds were 2.0e-13 and 12.63 at n = 1 and 5.0e-7 and 14.51 at n = 2, for 3.9e-13 and 25.26, and 1.0e-6 and 29.02. MATLAB BADS has 2*gammaincinv(p, v/2) (gppredcheck.m:20-22), the quantiles. The bounds are now chi2.ppf(p, n) (scipy.stats), and gammaincinv is no longer imported. With fewer than three statistics a refit is not possible at default options (it needs more than min_refit_time = 2D evaluations since the last refit, and each adds a statistic), so what changes in default runs is the poll's unreliable flag. Test: test_gaussian_process_train.py::test_gp_refit_time_chi_square_bounds, the verdicts (refit, unreliable) of the verifier's transcription of IsRefitTime and gppredcheck.m on z = 4 (n = 1), z = (3, 3.2), before and after min_refit_time, z = (6e-4, 6e-4) and z = (4, 4); at the parent it fails in the first four, where MATLAB's bounds accept z = 4 and z = (3, 3.2), which the halved ones flagged, and flag z = (6e-4, 6e-4), which they accepted. Fingerprint 2ca10868bed8a68e, as at the parent: the six runs of the fingerprint meet no sum of squares between the two bounds, while the verifier found default runs at every uncertainty level reaching the test at n = 2 (B5 verifier, I-F5); gated with W1-4 and W1-3 by the population comparison.
