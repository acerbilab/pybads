fix: without poll training, the poll records no refit of the GP that it does not make (W1-8)

With poll_training off, the poll asked _is_gp_refit_time_ whether a refit was due, which records a due refit (lastfitgp set to the evaluation count, the GP statistics reset, through _record_gp_refit_), and after the first iteration then cancelled the refit: the refit was recorded but not made, so that the search could refit no sooner than min_refit_time evaluations later, and its refit period counted from the reset. MATLAB BADS has the same order (bads.m:822-823, IsRefitTime then PollTraining), a defect it shares; the option is on by default. _is_gp_refit_time_ now takes refit_allowed, which the poll passes false after the first iteration without poll training: no refit is due and none is recorded, and the calibration of the GP is checked all the same, so that the poll's stopping rule reads the verdict on the statistics it keeps. The refit forced after a failed rebuild is unchanged: the poll makes it only with poll_training on, or in the first iteration. The stub of _is_gp_refit_time_ in the probe of test_gp_update_failures.py takes the new argument. On the verifier's 3-D ellipsoid (v10_poll_training_off.py, seed 70) the refits recorded and made go from 9 and 3 to 9 and 9. Test: test_gp_update_failures.py::test_poll_without_poll_training_records_no_refit, which fails at the parent (5 recorded, 3 made). Fingerprint bfbc6d6737e99d88, unchanged: default runs keep poll training on.
