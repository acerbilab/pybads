fix: gp_mean_fun accepts only the constant and the zero mean, refused otherwise when BADS is created (W1-34)

_init_optim_state_ accepted 12 names of gp_mean_fun, of which only "zero", "const" and "negquad" can be built (_meanfun_name_to_mean_function): the other 9 passed the check and stopped the run at initialization with "Unknown mean function!". "negquad" is PyVBMC's concave mean for log densities, the wrong shape for a minimizer; _gp_hyp gives it no priors, and the run stops at the first fit retry. MATLAB BADS has only the constant mean (gpdefBads.m, meanConst). The check now accepts "const" and "zero" and refuses every other name, "negquad" included, with a message that says what is accepted; the option's description names the two. _meanfun_name_to_mean_function and the mean branches of gaussian_process_train.py are unchanged. Tests: test_gp_mean_fun_refused ("negquad" and "se", which BADS accepted at the parent) and test_gp_mean_fun_accepted ("zero" and "const" build the GP with their mean), in test_gaussian_process_train.py, whose BADS helper now takes options.
