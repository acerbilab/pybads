fix: GP priors taken from the spread of equal targets fall back to a positive width or the previous centre (W1-26)

A target whose lowest 80% of initial values were equal (a penalty plateau over the plausible box) stopped the run at the initial fit: _gp_hyp set the SD of the mean's prior to np.std(hpd_y) = 0, which gpyreg's set_priors refuses with ValueError. At a rebuild, local_gp_fitting centred the output scale's prior at log(np.std(gp.y)), -inf when the local targets are equal, which set_priors refuses the same way. MATLAB's definition prior of the mean is N(0, 1) (gpdefBads.m:171), and it fits no GP at initialization; at a rebuild it takes log(std(y)) as it comes (gpdefBads.m:293). The mean's prior in _gp_hyp now takes the SD 1, which the code already took for a single target, whenever the targets have no spread; at a rebuild, the output scale's prior keeps its previous centre, as the mean's prior keeps its previous width on a zero range (KD-B6-2). Wave 0's thin feasible region (non_box_cons |x1 - x2| <= 0.005, "Found while verifying") is the second case: the initial design is infeasible, the local GP holds x0 alone, and its rebuild met log(0). Tests in test_gaussian_process_train.py: test_plateau_initial_design_runs (D = 3, 1e3 outside a small ball), test_rebuild_with_equal_targets_keeps_output_scale_prior (with and without a refit) and test_thin_feasible_region_runs (D = 3).
