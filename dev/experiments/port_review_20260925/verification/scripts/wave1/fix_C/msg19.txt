fix: the number of starting points of a GP fit stays in range on a budget no larger than the initial design (W1-19)

_get_gp_training_options sets init_N by a cubic that falls from gp_train_n_init (128) to gp_train_n_init_final (8) over the fraction of the budget used after the initial design, (n_eff - eff_starting_points) / (min(max_fun_evals, n_train_max) - eff_starting_points). When that budget equalled the initial design, 0/0 gave NaN and round raised ValueError; below it, the fraction was negative and the cubic extrapolated above 128 (413 and 968 at D = 2 and 3). The schedule is PyBADS's own (MATLAB starts a refit from its previous hyperparameters, W1-15). The fraction is now clipped to [0, 1], and a budget no larger than the initial design counts as used up (fraction 1, init_N = gp_train_n_init_final), so init_N stays between the two options. The cubic's lambda, which mixed its argument x_ with the enclosing x, now uses x_ alone, which changes no value. Test: test_gaussian_process_train.py::test_get_gp_training_options_small_budget, D = 2 and 3 with max_fun_evals 2 to 5, the budgets of the B5 verifier's v14_small_budget.py (that these runs make more evaluations than max_fun_evals is slice B2's item, left).
