fix: an iterate whose re-estimate fails drops out of the choices, and the incumbent keeps its estimate (W1-35)

_re_evaluate_history_ (W0-1) sets the hyperparameters recorded at each iterate on a copy of the working GP with compute_posterior=False, rebuilds it around the iterate with local_gp_fitting (no refit) and predicts. When the rebuild's posterior fails, local_gp_fitting puts back the GP it was given, which has no posterior, and marks it needs_refit; predict then raised TypeError, which crashed every noisy run of gp_update_failures.py --inject 0.02 (default runs do not reach it). MATLAB BADS records NaN for such an iterate (reevaluateIterList, bads.m:1378-1413: a failed gpupdate clears post, private/gpupdate.m:340-349, and gppred recomputes it, fails and keeps its NaN, utils/gppred.m:22-56), and its max and min skip NaN (bads.m:1108, 1144), where NumPy's argmax and argmin pick it. As the PI ruled, a failed rebuild, told by the needs_refit marker on the GP that local_gp_fitting returns, gives a past iterate NaN fval and fsd, as in MATLAB, while the current iterate, the last row, keeps the estimate it was recorded with, so that the incumbent is never NaN (MATLAB's becomes NaN there, bads.m:1101-1104). The choice of a better iterate at the end of an iteration takes np.nanargmax (a NaN improvement is no improvement) and the final choice np.nanargmin. The stall tests are unchanged: a NaN base gives no stall stop in that iteration, as in MATLAB. The last row is the incumbent's at both calls: at the end of an iteration, the call follows the recording of row poll_iteration from the incumbent, and rows are recorded only at poll_iteration, which never decreases; the loop ends only by is_finished, whose pass records that row, and the only change to the incumbent after it, the switch to a better iterate, comes with no new evaluation, so that the call at the end of the run then returns at once (last_re_eval). Over 40 noisy runs (levels 1 and 2, seeds 0-9, 150 evaluations, with and without failures of the rebuild), the last row held the incumbent's u, yval, fval and fsd at each of the 337 re-estimates that ran (315 at the end of an iteration, 22 at the end of the run). Neither slice can be all NaN: both hold the last row (poll_iteration >= 1 at both calls), whose estimate is the incumbent's, so no fallback is added.
