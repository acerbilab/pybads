fix: optim_state["plb"] and ["pub"] hold the lower and the upper plausible bound, not the reverse (W1-1)

_init_optim_state_ stored the transformed lower plausible bound in optim_state["pub"] and the upper one in optim_state["plb"]. Their only reader, the bounded limits of the poll scale in local_gp_fitting, takes them in place of infinite bounds, so on a problem unbounded in every variable (PyBADS refuses a mix of bounded and unbounded variables) the width ub - lb was -2 in every variable and capped poll_scale at -2: the ES-ell search, which scales by it, was isotropic whatever the length scales. The poll divides its directions by poll_scale and multiplies them back, so it was unaffected. The two assignments are swapped, as MATLAB stores them (private/setupvars.m:67-68) and reads them (private/gpupdate.m:301-308). No other code reads the two keys (the two tests of _get_fevals_data draw dummy points between them, which holds either way). AGENTS.md, "Two coordinate spaces", stated the swap as a fact; it now says that optim_state["lb"], ["ub"], ["plb"] and ["pub"] hold the transformed bounds, read by gaussian_process_train.py.

test_poll_scale_follows_length_scales_when_unbounded (test_gaussian_process_train.py): after the initial design of an unbounded 3-D problem, a refit's poll_scale is positive and equals the normalized length scales clipped to the width of the plausible box, 2 in normalized units (it was [-2, -2, -2]). Bounded runs do not reach the change: fingerprint c0639f00f056a030, that of the parent.
