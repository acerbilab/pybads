fix: refuse a hedge_beta that is not a finite number at least 0 and a hedge_decay outside [0, 1] when BADS is created (W4-29)

hedge_beta and hedge_decay were not checked, and every bad value failed
silently, the run completing. The hedge chooses a search method with
probabilities from softmax(hedge_beta * g), g the searches' gains, and
multiplies the gains by hedge_decay at each update (search_hedge.py, as
searchHedge.m:45 and acqPortfolio.m:69, which do not check them either;
bads.m:289-290 gives the defaults, 1e-3/TolFun and 0.1^(1/(2*nvars))). A
negative hedge_beta inverted the hedge, so that the search of lower gain
was the likelier; at -1000 the softmax overflowed, and at inf or NaN it
multiplied a zero, so that the probabilities and the gains were NaN and
every choice was the uniform fallback. A hedge_decay above 1 made the
gains grow geometrically until they overflowed, after which every choice
was random; a negative one made them alternate in sign; NaN made them
NaN from the first update.

_init_optim_state_ now refuses, with ValueError, a hedge_beta that is not
a finite number at least 0 and a hedge_decay that is not a number from 0
to 1, beside the check of hedge_gamma and in its form: a bool, a string,
a complex number, NaN and an array of several values are refused. 0
stays accepted for both (a uniform choice, and a decay that keeps only
the last reward), and so does a hedge_decay of 1 (no decay). The message
for hedge_beta names its default, 1e-3 / options['tol_fun'], which a
negative tol_fun makes negative. The defaults pass at every D: hedge_beta
is 1 at the default tol_fun, and hedge_decay = 0.1**(1/(2*D)) lies in
[0.1**0.5, 1). The descriptions of both options give their range.
