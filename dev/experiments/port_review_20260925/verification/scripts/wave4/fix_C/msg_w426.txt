fix: the output function's "done" call receives the final estimate of a noisy run in optim_state (W4-26)

The final estimate of a noisy run sets self.u, self.yval, self.fval and
self.fsd from the iterate it chooses, and fval and fsd then from the
final samples, but left optim_state's "u", "yval", "fval" and "fsd" at
the last iteration's values, so the output function's "done" call
received them beside the returned x. MATLAB BADS's optimState is stale
there too (FinalEstimate writes iterList.fval and iterList.fsd at the
chosen index, bads.m:1150-1165, not optimState.fval), and no result
reads these entries after the loop.

After the final estimate, optim_state takes the returned point and its
values, as the re-estimation at the end of an iteration keeps them in
step (W3-33), in both of the final estimate's cases: after a later
iteration, and within the first (W4-14). A run without uncertainty
handling keeps optim_state in step through _update_incumbent_, as
before. iteration_history already holds the final estimate at the
chosen iterate, as MATLAB's iterList does: in ten seeded noisy runs at
D = 3, with and without specify_target_noise, and two that end in their
first iteration, fval and fsd at the returned iterate are the result's.

Test: test_noisy_runs.py::test_final_estimate_keeps_optim_state_in_step,
with and without specify_target_noise: an output function captures
optim_state at "done", whose u is the returned point and whose yval,
fval and fsd are the incumbent's and the result's. At the parent both
fail: without target noise optim_state's fval is 0.169, the result's
-0.163, and with it optim_state's u is not the returned point.
