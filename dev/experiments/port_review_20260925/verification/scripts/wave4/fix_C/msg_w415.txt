fix: with opp_stobads, an uncertain poll moves only to a polled point that improves on the incumbent (W4-15)

With stobads and opp_stobads, a poll without a success and with an
uncertain point moved the incumbent to u_poll_best, the polled point of
the largest positive improvement, and marked the poll as moved. When no
polled point improved on the incumbent, u_poll_best was still the
incumbent itself, so the poll "moved" the incumbent to itself and was
marked as moved, and every search of the next round rebuilt the local
GP (the reset after a moved poll), search_n_try - 1 rebuilds more than
an unmarked poll gives. The comment above the branch, and the
changelog, say that such a poll moves to its best polled point.

The branch now also requires poll_best_improvement > 0: an uncertain
poll moves, and is marked as moved, only to a polled point that
improves on the incumbent; otherwise it neither moves nor is marked, as
a failed poll. Sto-BADS has no counterpart in MATLAB BADS, whose poll
moves only on a positive improvement (bads.m:951-958). Whether an
uncertain outcome that does not improve should move at all is W0-13's
question, which this leaves open.

Tests: test_stobads.py::
test_uncertain_poll_without_improvement_does_not_move, new: an
uncertain first poll whose points are all scripted as worse than the
incumbent leaves it in place and unmarked; at the parent it moves it to
itself. test_uncertain_poll_moves_only_with_opp_stobads asserted only
that _update_incumbent_ was called, which the self-move satisfied (in
its seeded run every polled point is worse): it now scripts an
uncertain point that improves and asserts that the incumbent moves to
it and the poll is marked, with opp_stobads only. The helper
_scripted_first_poll scripts the improvements and records the
incumbent and the mark.
