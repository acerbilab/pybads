fix: poll_scale and effective_radius over several hyperparameter samples, as MATLAB BADS weights them (W4-16)

After a refit, local_gp_fitting computes the geometry of the local GP
over its hyperparameter samples. len_scale was their mean, MATLAB's sum
weighted by hypweight with equal weights, but poll_scale summed the
centred log length scales over the samples without the weights, which
gives MATLAB's value raised to the number of samples, and
effective_radius was one value per sample, where gpupdate.m takes one
radius from the weighted mean of the shape alpha (gpupdate.m:294-299,
313-317). No option or path gives the GP more than one sample today
(_robust_gp_fit_ returns one row, and gp_s_N is 0 at both call sites),
so no run was affected.

poll_scale now weights the centred log length scales by 1/N, and the
radius comes from the mean shape, one value, as the initial
effective_radius of 1.0 that _gp_hyp sets. With one sample the
division by 1 and the sum of one element are exact, so nothing moves.

Test: test_gaussian_process_train.py::
test_rebuild_geometry_is_weighted_over_samples, beside the test of
len_scale: a refit forced to return two samples (the O verifier's
v_ok1_samples.py, part B), whose poll_scale and effective_radius must
equal a transcription of gpupdate.m:294-299 and 313-317 with hypweight
1/2. At the parent poll_scale is the transcription's squared
([3.630, 0.275] against [1.905, 0.525]) and effective_radius has two
values. The verifier's part B gives MATLAB's values with the fix.
