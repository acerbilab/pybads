fix: a noisy run that ends in its first iteration takes its reserved final samples, at the incumbent (W4-14)

A noisy run reserves noise_final_samples evaluations of max_fun_evals
for the final estimate at the returned point (_init_optimization_), but
optimize took them only after a later iteration (poll_iteration > 0),
as MATLAB BADS's iter > 1 (bads.m:1138), whose choice among the
iterates skips the first. A run that ended within its first iteration,
on max_iter=1 or on a small budget (at D = 2, any max_fun_evals up to
48, since the initial design has 32 points), left the reserved
evaluations unused and reported the incumbent's single observation in
yval_vec, with the GP's fsd, which at level 1 is the default
noise_size.

Such a run has one iterate, the incumbent, and it now takes the
reserved samples there: fval and fsd are their mean and its standard
error (their precision-weighted mean with specify_target_noise), with
yval_vec and ysd_vec, and the estimate is recorded at that iterate of
iteration_history, as the final estimate after a later iteration does.
The choice among the iterates and the re-evaluation of the history are
unchanged for later iterations. A run that output_fcn stops in its
initialization, whose loop never ran, still takes no final samples.
This departs from MATLAB BADS, which shares the gap.

Tests: test_noisy_runs.py::
test_small_noisy_budget_takes_the_reserved_final_samples, a run at
D = 2 with max_fun_evals=38 (the B7 verifier's v4_noisy_budget.py):
it ends within its first iteration, takes its 4 reserved samples at the
returned point and makes 38 evaluations; at the parent it makes 34.
test_noisy_run_in_one_iteration_takes_the_final_samples, formerly
test_noisy_run_in_one_iteration_reports_incumbent, which asserted the
old behavior, now asserts the new one at max_iter=1, for both kinds of
noise; at the parent yval_vec has shape (1,), not (10,).
