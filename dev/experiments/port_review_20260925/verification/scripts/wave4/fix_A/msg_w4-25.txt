fix: refuse an n_search_iter that is not a positive integer when BADS is created (W4-25)

n_search_iter, the number of generations of the ES search, was not
checked, and a wrong value stopped the run at its first search, after
the initial design: 0 with ZeroDivisionError (search_hedge.py divides
n_search by it), 0.5, 2.5 or inf with TypeError (es_search.py loops over
range(n_search_iter)), -1 with NumPy's ValueError "negative dimensions
are not allowed", "2" with TypeError; True ran as 1. MATLAB BADS does not
check it either (setupoptions.m only evaluates it; searchES.m:125 loops
over 1:Nsearchiter).

_init_optim_state_ refuses, with ValueError, an n_search_iter that is not
a positive integer, beside the check of accelerate_mesh_steps and in its
form: a bool, a non-numeric value, NaN and inf are refused, and a
whole-number float is converted to int.

Tests (test_bads_inputs.py): test_n_search_iter_not_a_positive_integer_is_refused
(0, -1, 0.5, 2.5, NaN, inf, True and "2"), which fails at the parent in
all 8 cases (no ValueError), and
test_n_search_iter_positive_integer_is_accepted (1, 2, 3.0 and
np.int64(4), stored as int, and a run of 60 evaluations), which fails at
the parent at 3.0 and np.int64(4).

Fingerprint (Linux, one BLAS thread, gpyreg v1.3.3): 360971bf1f0ba6cb,
unchanged.
