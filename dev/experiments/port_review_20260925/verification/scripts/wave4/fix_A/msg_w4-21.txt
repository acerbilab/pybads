fix: bin the candidates of contraints_check and split the ES search's first population with MATLAB's round (W4-21)

contraints_check binned the candidates and the evaluated points on a grid
of tol_mesh / 2 with np.round, which takes an exact half to the even
integer, where uCheck.m:22 and 24 use MATLAB's round, which takes it away
from zero. From the poll mesh 2^-6 at default the search grid is finer
than a bin, and its points fall on halves of a bin: the port then merged
other candidates into one bin, and removed other candidates as
evaluated, than uCheck.m does. The ES search split its first population
between its two scales with the same rounding (searchES.m:111), which
differs when n_search / n_search_iter is odd, never at default: at
n_search_iter = 3 the port gave [682, 683] and MATLAB gives [683, 682].

The exact rule of force_to_grid (np.modf, the integer part moved away
from zero when the fraction is at least one half in magnitude) becomes
round_half_away, in a module of its own, pybads/rounding.py, used by
force_to_grid (whose results it leaves as they were), by the bins of
contraints_check and by the split of ESSearch. It lives outside
pybads.search because constraints_check.py cannot import that package:
pybads.search imports es_search, which imports contraints_check, and
pybads.bads imports pybads.function_logger first. The comment above the
bins says what the code does.

Tests (test_search.py): test_constraint_check_rounds_halves_of_a_bin_away_from_zero,
candidates half a bin from an evaluated point on both sides of zero, one
bin from it, and pairs of candidates half a bin apart, against a
transcription of uCheck.m with MATLAB's round and against the rows it
returns; test_es_search_splits_its_first_population_as_matlab, the split
at n_search_iter 2, 3, 7 and 4096. At the parent, the first fails and the
second fails at 3, 7 and 4096. The doublecheck's random comparison with
uCheck.m (c2_ucheck_random.py) now keeps the same bins in 3000 of 3000
sets on grids finer than a bin (1748 of 3000 before); the rows differ in
1075 of them only by the candidate kept within a bin, the first in input
order where setdiff keeps the smallest (W3-1).
