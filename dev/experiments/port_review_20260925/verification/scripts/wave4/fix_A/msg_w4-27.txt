fix: log a generation of the ES search that leaves no candidate at DEBUG, not as a warning (W4-27)

The ES search logged "No candidate left in generation k of the search,
..." as a warning when the constraints and the points already evaluated
removed every candidate of a generation, which happens a few times per
run on a thin band; MATLAB's searchES.m is silent there, and the search
goes on with the candidates of the earlier generations, as it does. The
message is logged at DEBUG, which display="full" shows.

Test: test_es_search_keeps_the_candidates_of_an_emptied_generation
(test_empty_search.py) sets the BADS logger to DEBUG and checks that the
message is logged once, at DEBUG; at the parent it fails in both of its
cases, the record at WARNING (30 != 10).
