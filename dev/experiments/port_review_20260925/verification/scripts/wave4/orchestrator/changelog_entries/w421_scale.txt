**Scale of the evolution-strategy search.** With `n_search_iter` of 3 or more, the scale of each generation of the evolution-strategy search follows the fraction of new candidates among the best, counted as in MATLAB BADS; PyBADS counted older candidates as new from the third generation on, so that the scale grew faster than that of MATLAB BADS, and from about the fifth generation grew where that of MATLAB BADS shrinks. When a generation has an odd number of candidates (`n_search` over `n_search_iter`, rounded down), the first generation draws the extra candidate at the smaller of its two scales, as MATLAB BADS does, where PyBADS drew it at the larger. The default `n_search_iter`, 2, is affected by neither.
