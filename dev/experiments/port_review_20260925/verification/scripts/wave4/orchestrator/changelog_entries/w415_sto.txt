**Sto-BADS poll.** With `stobads=True`, a poll succeeds when one of its points succeeds, as in Sto-MADS, and moves the incumbent to that point; before, the outcome of the last polled point decided, so that a success followed by other points was lost and the mesh contracted. With `opp_stobads`, a poll without a success moves to its best point when that point improves on the incumbent and any of the poll's points is uncertain, not only when the last one is.
