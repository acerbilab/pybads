**Noise test in the schedule of the GP's fits.** The noise test, the second evaluation of the starting point when `uncertainty_handling` is left empty, no longer counts in the schedule of the hyperparameter fits of the Gaussian process, whose number of starting points follows the fraction of the budget used after the initial design; in 1.1.0 the schedule ran one evaluation ahead. The noise test still counts in `max_fun_evals` and `func_count`. Results change at default options.
