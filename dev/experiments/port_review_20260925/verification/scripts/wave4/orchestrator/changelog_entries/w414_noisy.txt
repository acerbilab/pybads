**Noisy runs that end within their first iteration.** A run with uncertainty handling that ends within its first iteration, for instance with `max_iter=1`, returns its result instead of raising `KeyError: 'yval_vec'`. Such a run, which a `max_fun_evals` that the initial design nearly uses up also gives, takes the final samples that it reserves from `max_fun_evals` at the incumbent, as a longer run takes them at the point it returns: `fval` and `fsd` are their estimate, and `yval_vec` and `ysd_vec` hold them. MATLAB BADS takes none then.
