docs: cache_size is the initial size of the log of evaluations, which grows when it is full (W4-12)

The description of cache_size, "Size of cache for storing fcn
evaluations", read as a bound on the log, but cache_size is only the
number of rows that FunctionLogger allocates at the start: when they are
full, it adds half as many rows as it holds (_expand_arrays), and no
evaluation is dropped. (MATLAB BADS keeps a ring of CacheSize rows that
overwrites its oldest ones, funlogger.m:120-121; the two agree up to 9999
logged evaluations at MATLAB's default of 1e4.) The description, which
the options page shows, now says so.
