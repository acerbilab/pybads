fix: the initial design is seeded from the run's generator, so that random_seed decides it (W4-1)

init_sobol seeded scipy's scrambled Sobol set from the characters of
array2string(u0[:11].astype(uint64)): every coordinate inside the
plausible box, (-1, 1) in u, has the integer part 0, so every start
inside the box gave one design per D (seed 967 at D = 3; 1 from D = 8,
where the int64 product of the characters is 0), whatever random_seed.
A coordinate at or below -1 (x0 on or below its plausible lower bound)
reached the cast of a negative float to uint64, which is undefined in C
and differs between x86-64 and AArch64 (W4-2). The branch that drew the
seed from the run's generator was unreachable, since BADS replaces a
non-finite x0 first. So the scrambling drew from a generator that the
run's seed did not reach, against the statements that every random draw
of a run comes from bads.rng.

As ruled (W4-1 (a)), the seed of the scrambling is now one draw of the
generator that init_sobol receives, bads.rng, and the seed from u0 goes,
with its cast to uint64 (and W4-2's platform dependence with it). This
departs from MATLAB BADS, whose initSobol.m:9-15 derives a skip index
into the unscrambled sequence from num2str(u0), so that its design
depends on the start alone. The draw covers [0, 2**63): the range
[1, 997] of the old branch is MATLAB's bound on its skip index
(MaxSeed = 997), which means nothing for a scrambling seed and would
allow only 997 designs per D. One draw rather than the generator itself
passed to scipy's Sobol: the run's generator then advances by exactly one
draw at the design, whatever scipy's Sobol draws for its scrambling,
which depends on D and on scipy's implementation. init_sobol's docstring
and AGENTS.md's sentence on the design's seed (in "Randomness goes
through one numpy.random.Generator") say so.

Tests: test_init_sobol.py::
test_init_sobol_design_follows_the_generator_not_the_start, at D = 3:
four starts, two inside the plausible box and two on its bounds, give
one design for one generator seed, and two seeds give two designs; at
the parent the starts on the bounds give another design, and the two
seeds the same one. test_bads_seed.py::test_seed_decides_initial_design,
the same through BADS (the log's rows of the design in a run of 10
evaluations): starts at x0 = 4, at (-5, 0, 10) and on the plausible lower
bound give one design at random_seed 42, and 43 gives another; at the
parent the start on the bound gives another design, and 42 and 43 the
same one. Both fail at the parent and pass here.
