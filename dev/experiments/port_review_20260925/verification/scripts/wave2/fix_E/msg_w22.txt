fix: a variable bounded on one side only is accepted, as in MATLAB BADS (W2-2)

_bounds_check_ refused a variable with one finite and one infinite
hard bound (bads:HalfBounds, "Variables bounded only below/above are
not supported"). MATLAB BADS accepts it: boundscheck.m has no such
test, setupvars.m:27-38 only prints a caution for infinite bounds, and
bads.m documents LB(i) = -Inf and UB(i) = Inf separately. The refusal
could not be lifted while the effective bounds replaced the infinite
range by 1e3 and moved x0 and plb by a unit (W2-4, two commits back).

The refusal is removed. The rest of the setup and of the run already
takes the bounds variable by variable: VariableTransformer maps an
infinite bound to an infinite u, and log-transforms a variable with
ub = inf whose other bounds are positive and pub / plb >= 10, as
transvars.m does (bit for bit, B1-C); lb_search and ub_search stay
infinite on that side; the checks of the search and the poll compare
elementwise; W1-1's geometry in gaussian_process_train.py takes the
plausible bound in place of an infinite one for the poll scale, and
the GP's maximum length scale is capped at 100 for an infinite range.
The docstring of BADS says that lb[i] = -inf and ub[i] = inf are set
separately, and that plb (pub) needs to be given when lb (ub) has an
infinite bound, since a missing plausible bound defaults to the hard
one; the warning for infinite bounds also names the variables that
have one.
