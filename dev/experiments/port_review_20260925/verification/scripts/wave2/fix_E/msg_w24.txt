fix: the start and the plausible bounds are used as given, without effective bounds 1e-3 of the range inside the hard bounds (W2-4)

_bounds_check_ computed "effective" bounds 1e-3 of the linear range
inside each finite hard bound (1e-3 of 1e3 for an infinite range),
refused hard bounds that this margin left empty, moved an x0 within the
margin inside it, moved plb and pub inside it, and expanded the
plausible box to an x0 that lay within the margin. MATLAB BADS never
moves x0 or the plausible bounds: boundscheck.m sets PLB = LB and
PUB = UB when they are omitted, and setupvars.m tests the order
LB <= PLB < PUB <= UB (lines 6-9) and that the start is within the hard
bounds (lines 95-99), then puts it on the grid. So with plb and pub
omitted PyBADS lost part of the plausible box (three decades of
[1e-3, 1e3]), [1, 10] lost its log transform (9.991 / 1.009 < 10), a
start at the optimum 0.01 of [1e-3, 1e3] started at 1.001, and
[0, 1] with plb = 1e-4, pub = 5e-4 was refused.

The block is removed, and MATLAB's two checks stay: x0 within the hard
bounds, and the order of the bounds, once (the first of its two copies
went with the block, and with it the message that said < where the
test is <=). A start outside the plausible box is kept where it is and
the box is not expanded to it, as in MATLAB (the direction that the
internal review's I-F3 proposed is not adopted: outside the margin
PyBADS already equalled MATLAB).

Test: test_bads_inputs.py::test_start_and_plausible_bounds_are_kept,
eight cases of one variable (plausible bounds omitted, a start on a
hard bound, a start within the old margin outside the plausible box,
[1, 10] and [1e-3, 1e3] log-transformed, plausible bounds close to a
hard bound on either side), each with MATLAB's start, plausible bounds,
log flag, transformed hard bounds and start on the grid, as the B1
verifier's transcription of boundscheck.m and setupvars.m gives them
(v_bounds.out). All 8 fail at the parent (6 on a moved value, 2
refused) and pass here.
