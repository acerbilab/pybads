fix: one final sample gives a yval_vec of shape (2,) at level 1 and a number in the final message at level 2 (W2-34)

With one final sample and no noise SD from the target, the sample and the
incumbent's observation `yval` were stacked by `np.vstack` into a
`yval_vec` of shape (2, 1), where every other `yval_vec` of a result has
shape (n,) and MATLAB BADS's is a 1x2 row (`[yval_vec, yval]`,
bads.m:1463-1466). They are joined by `np.append`, shape (2,); the mean
and standard error computed from them are the same.

With one final sample and the target's noise SD (level 2), the final
message printed the one-element array (`[1.3578806]`) where MATLAB prints
the number (`yval_vec(1)`, bads.m:1175-1176). It prints the element.

Tests, in test_noisy_runs.py:
::test_one_final_sample_without_target_noise_adds_the_incumbent (at the
parent `assert (2, 1) == (2,)`) and
::test_final_message_from_one_sample_prints_a_number (at the parent the
message reads "Observed function value at minimum: [1.3578806] (1
sample). ..."); both pass at this commit.
