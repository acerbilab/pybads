fix: the display's Actions column shows what each poll's pass did (W2-31)

The poll appended "Train" (with " (failed)") to `logging_action` only
when the pass refitted the GP, and "Skip" only when the poll was skipped,
and the display printed the last entry: a poll that did neither showed
the entry of an earlier poll, a stale "Train" (12 of 13 polls in the
reviewer's run with refits suppressed after the first ones, 1 in 8 to 3
in 11 at default options), and one that trained and was skipped showed
"Skip" alone. MATLAB BADS builds `action` anew at each poll
(bads.m:1024-1029): "Train", " (failed)" when the fit failed, then "Skip"
or ", skip". The poll now builds its action string in the same way and
always appends it, empty when the pass did neither.

Tests, in test_bads_logger.py, on the poll lines of a `display="iter"`
run: ::test_poll_actions_without_a_refit (refits allowed only at the
first three checks, no searches; at the parent the line of the second
poll ends in a stale "Train") and
::test_poll_actions_of_a_poll_that_trains_and_skips (skipping allowed
from the first failed poll step; at the parent the first poll, which
refits and is skipped, shows "Skip"); both pass at this commit.
