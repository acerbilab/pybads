fix: overhead counts the time of the final samples and of merged repeats in the target's time (W2-20)

`overhead` divides the run's time by the target's,
`FunctionLogger.total_fun_eval_time`, to which only an evaluation at a new
point added: neither an evaluation with `record_duplicate_data=False`
(the final samples of a noisy run) nor a repeat merged into its row at
level 2 (`specify_target_noise`) did, so a noisy run's overhead was
overstated (2.59 for 1.99 in the verifier's run). MATLAB's funlogger adds
the time of every 'iter' and 'single' call (private/funlogger.m:130), and
its noise test at `x0` calls the target directly, untimed
(private/evalinitmesh.m:41).

`FunctionLogger._record` now adds the time of every evaluation to the
total, whatever becomes of its point, and `_init_mesh_` leaves the noise
test's time out of it, as MATLAB does. Only `overhead` changes; the
per-row times and the evaluation counts are as before.

Tests: test_function_logger.py::test_record_duplicate_adds_its_time_to_the_total
(a level-2 merge and two unrecorded evaluations; at the parent
`assert 1.0 == 15.0`) and
test_noisy_runs.py::test_target_time_counts_every_evaluation_but_the_noise_test,
a 60-evaluation run on a noisy target with the noise test and 10 final
samples, with the logger's timer replaced by one that times every call at
1 s, so that the count is exact: the total is `func_count - 1`, all but
the noise test (at the parent `assert 49.0 == (60 - 1)`, the final
samples missing).
