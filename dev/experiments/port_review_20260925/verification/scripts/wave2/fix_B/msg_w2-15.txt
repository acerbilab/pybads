fix: the display option follows MATLAB BADS's display levels, "notify" and "final" included (W2-15)

display was compared as an exact string (bads.py): "off" set the BADS
logger to WARNING, "iter" to INFO and "full" to DEBUG, and every other
value, "notify" and "final" included, which the option's description
lists, and "none", "OFF" or "Iter", left it at INFO, the full display.
MATLAB BADS reads the first three letters, lower case (bads.m:312-328):
off/none 0, notify 1, final 2, iter/all 3, and any other value 1; it
prints its opening line from 1 on (evalinitmesh.m:52), the iteration
lines at 3 (evalinitmesh.m:65-83, bads.m:1022) and its final message from
2 on (bads.m:1172).

The logger's level is now set from the first three letters of display,
lower case: "off" and "none" WARNING, "notify" (and any value not listed,
as in MATLAB) the opening message's level, "final" the final message's,
"iter" and "all" INFO, and "full", PyBADS's own, DEBUG, as before. The
opening message ("Beginning optimization of a ... objective function") is
logged at 25, the level of the message of a random starting point, which
MATLAB also prints from 1 on (setupvars.m:80), and the final message at
22, both between the iteration lines (INFO) and the warnings. The option's
description names "full".
