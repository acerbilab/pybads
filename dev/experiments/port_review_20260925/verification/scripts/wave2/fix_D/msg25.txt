fix: the re-estimation's move takes the incumbent's location with its value (W2-25)

At the end of an iteration of a noisy run, when the re-estimation finds an
earlier iterate better by more than tol_fun, the incumbent took that
iterate's yval, fval and fsd, and its location went to self.u and to
self.best_u, a name nothing reads. u_best stayed at the old incumbent, and
the next pass set self.u = self.u_best, so the next search's target was
predicted at the old incumbent, and a poll that no successful search
preceded ran around the old incumbent, judged by the chosen iterate's
value; optim_state's incumbent was not updated either. MATLAB BADS does the
same (bads.m:1111-1118 sets u and not ubest; u = ubest at bads.m:769),
and the port matched it only because best_u is dead. By the PI's ruling
(option (b)), this departs from MATLAB: the move now calls
_update_incumbent_ with the iterate's u, yval, fval and fsd, which sets
u_best and optim_state's u, yval, fval and fsd as a search or a poll does,
and the dead best_u line goes. The target's hyperparameters still move to
the iterate and the working GP stays, as in MATLAB. Noisy runs change.

Test: test_noisy_runs.py::
test_re_estimation_moves_the_incumbent_with_its_value, a noisy sphere
(D = 2, noise SD 0.5, 200 evaluations, seed 0; 9 moves, 5 to another
location, 2 of them followed by a poll that no search moved): after each
move u_best and optim_state's u are the iterate's location, and the next
poll, when no search moved the incumbent first, is centred there. At the
parent, each of the three checks fails on its own.

Fingerprint: dc11118754b18b47, unchanged from the parent: its noisy runs
end at their 4th iteration and none moves before its last re-estimation,
after which only the final choice follows.
