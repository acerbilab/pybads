fix: ES-wcm takes one best training point per weight, floor(mu), as MATLAB BADS (W3-4)

ES-wcm computes its search covariance from the best training points of
the GP about the incumbent, mu = 0.5 times their number, with
floor(mu) log weights. The port took y_idx[0 : floor(mu + 1)], the
floor(mu) + 1 best points, one more than the weights and than MATLAB,
which takes index(1:floor(mu)) (search/searchES.m:62). Since ucov does
not weight the scatter (W3-3), the extra point entered the covariance
in full: the normalized search covariance differed from MATLAB's by 2
to 40% (median 11%, relative Frobenius) over the verifier's captured
states.

The slice is now y_idx[0 : floor(mu)], so the point count equals the
number of weights, as in MATLAB. This changes every ES-wcm search, at
default options.

Test: test_search.py::test_es_wcm_takes_one_best_point_per_weight: the
rows that ES-wcm passes to ucov after the initial design (17 training
points) are the floor(17 / 2) = 8 best, as many as the weights. It
fails at the parent (assert 9 == 8) and passes here.
