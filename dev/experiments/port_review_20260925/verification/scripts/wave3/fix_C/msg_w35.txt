fix: each parent of the ES gets the offspring MATLAB BADS gives it, not those of the parent before (W3-5)

The ES reproduces its ranked candidates with a selection mask, in which
parent k appears w_k times, w decreasing with the rank (MATLAB's
utils/ESupdate.m). MATLAB builds it from the 1-based start positions of
the parents' blocks, cw = cumsum(w) - w + 1, with idx(cw) = 1 and
selectmask = cumsum(idx(1:end-1)), whose values are 1-based parent
indices (ESupdate.m:19-21). The port used cw as 0-based positions, so
its mask was [0] followed by MATLAB's 1-based mask used as 0-based
indices: the best parent had one offspring and each other parent those
of the parent ranked before it (at mu = lambda = 2048, 1, 17, 12, 10, 9,
8 for the first six against MATLAB's 17, 12, 10, 9, 8, 7).

The mask is now np.repeat(np.arange(len(w)), w), MATLAB's 1-based
selectmask minus one: equal to it at every pair of mu and lambda from 1
to 59, and at the pairs of mu in 100, 1000, 1365, 2048, 4096 and lambda
in 100, 1024, 1365, 2048, 4096. The weights w are computed as before. This changes the reproduction of every search, at default
options.

Test: test_search.py::test_search_selection_mask, parametrized over
(mu, lambda) = (1, 2048), (2048, 2048), (100, 2048), (7, 20), (3, 3):
the offspring counts per parent equal the w of a transcription of
ESupdate.m, and the mask equals its selectmask minus one. It replaces
the golden sum 885072, which was the sum of MATLAB's 1-based mask and so
locked the shift in (the correct 0-based mask sums to 883024). The five
cases fail at the parent (at (1, 2048) the counts begin 1, 23, 17
against 23, 17, 14) and pass here.
