fix: contraints_check removes the candidates already evaluated, as MATLAB BADS (W3-1)

contraints_check, which the initial design, the search and the poll
call on their candidates, is meant to remove those already evaluated:
the candidates whose bin, of tol_mesh / 2, holds a point of the
function log. It stacked the candidates' bins above the log's and kept
the first occurrence of each unique bin among the candidates, but a
candidate's bin always occurs first among the candidates, since they
come first in the stack, so it removed no evaluated point: only
candidates that shared a bin with an earlier candidate. MATLAB's
uCheck removes them with setdiff(u1, u2, 'rows') (utils/uCheck.m:17-27).
Evaluated points were evaluated again: in seeded runs, the search at
level 0 when the optimum is on a bound or the grid is coarse, and the
poll at levels 1 and 2. At levels 0 and 1 a repeat is a new row of the
log and a duplicate training input of the GP; at level 2 it is merged
into its row. It matched MATLAB only in c7c88ab, and lost it in 8e59038.

The unique bins of the stack now also mark those that a logged point
maps to (np.unique's inverse), and the candidates kept are the first of
each bin that holds no logged point. The bins stay sorted, the order in
which setdiff returns them (W3-2), and the representative of a bin is
the first candidate of it, as before (MATLAB takes the smallest, a
difference below tol_mesh / 2). On 1000 random sets of candidates with
evaluated points among them, the result equals the verifier's
transcription of uCheck.m (its v_ucheck.py). A generation of the ES
emptied by the removal is skipped (W3-9).

Test: test_search.py::test_incumbent_constraint_check, corrected: it
asserted the old behavior (every candidate evaluated, only the
duplicate removed) and now asserts MATLAB's, every candidate removed;
its draws are now seeded. test_search.py::
test_constraint_check_removes_evaluated_points_as_matlab: eight
candidates, three of them in evaluated bins, one duplicate and one out
of bounds, give the three rows that uCheck.m returns, in its order.
Both fail at the parent (10 rows kept of 11 evaluated; 6 rows against
3) and pass here.
