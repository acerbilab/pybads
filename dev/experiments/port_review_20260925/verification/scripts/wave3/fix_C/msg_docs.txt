docs: the comments on the order of contraints_check and on the unweighted scatter of ES-wcm (W3-2, W3-3)

W3-2: the comment of contraints_check said that it removes the
duplicates "and preserve[s] the initial order". The duplicates step
does, but the removal of the evaluated vectors that follows returns the
rows in np.unique's order, sorted by their rounded coordinates, which
is the order of MATLAB's setdiff(u1, u2, 'rows') (utils/uCheck.m:25):
the verifier found the same order on 200 of 200 random sets. The
comment now says so.

W3-3: the comments of ES-wcm ("Compute weighted covariance matrix wrt
u0") and of ucov ("For broadcasting weighted sum") called the
covariance weighted. ucov sums each weight times the whole scatter,
which is the scatter times the sum of the weights, one for ES-wcm's,
as MATLAB's utils/ucov.m:19 does; by the PI's ruling (a) MATLAB's
arithmetic stays, and the comments now say that the weights do not
weight the scatter. black joins the line that the old inline comment
had split.
