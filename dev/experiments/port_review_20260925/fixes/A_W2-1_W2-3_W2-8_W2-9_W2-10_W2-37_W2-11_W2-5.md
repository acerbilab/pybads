## Noticed outside my rows
- **Integer bounds and the log transform (confirmed by a script).** `VariableTransformer` writes `np.log(...)` into the bound arrays in place, so integer-typed bounds are truncated. With int `lb=1, ub=1000, plb=2, pub=500`, the plausible box maps to [-0.769, 1.072] instead of [-1, 1]; the same bounds as floats give [-1, 1]. W2-3's broadcast keeps a scalar's dtype, so integer scalar bounds such as `lower_bounds=1` now meet this defect too, as integer vectors already did. The fix would be to cast the bounds to float, in `_bounds_check_` or in `VariableTransformer`.
- **List-valued plausible bounds.** A missing `x0` with list-valued `plausible_lower_bounds` still raises `AttributeError` (`.shape` in `__init__`), as the verifier noted.
- **The `bads:pbUnspecified` warning.** It is printed only just before the "plausible bounds need to be finite" error, because `__init__` fills in missing plausible bounds silently. MATLAB warns whenever it fills them in. This is relevant to W2-4.
- **Raises section.** It does not name the `bads:StartingSet` and `bads:NONBCON` refusals explicitly; they fall under "various checks for the bounds". I only documented `StartingSet` in the `x0` description.
- **Gridding after a redraw.** W2-11's loop checks the start before it is put on the grid, so a feasible draw near the constraint's boundary can still be refused after gridding, as before.
