## W1-3: `05a886b` fix: the calibration test of the GP reads the SD of the observation, not the latent SD (W1-3)

- **Files:** `pybads/bads/bads.py` (both `_save_gp_stats_` calls and `_is_gp_refit_time_`) and the test file.
- **What changed:** at the search and at the poll, the stored SD is now `sqrt(gp.predict(point, add_noise=True))` at the evaluated point, instead of `fs[index_acq]`. The 1e-6 replacement is removed.
  - The observation's SD is never zero, because `noise_log_scale` is bounded below by `log(tol_fun) - 1`.
  - At the search, the unused `fs` from `acq_fcn_lcb` became `_`.
- **Level 2 check:** MATLAB's `gppred` → `mygp` calls `likGaussHe(hyp.lik, [], [], fmu, fs2)` with s = []. So `ys2 = fs2 + exp(2*hyp.lik)`: the base noise only, without the target's SD. gpyreg's `add_noise=True` with `s2_star=None` adds exactly the constant noise (the user-provided term is 0), so it does the same.
- **Test:** `test_gp_stats_hold_sd_of_observation[0]` and `[2]` (D = 3, 60 evaluations each). Every stored SD must equal the square root of the latent variance at the point plus `exp(2*noise_log_scale) * sn2_mult` (rtol 1e-10).
  - At the parent: `2 failed`.
    - Level 0: `Mismatched elements: 54 / 54 (100%)`, `Max relative difference among violations: 3.68741961`.
    - Level 2: `Mismatched elements: 16 / 17 (94.1%)`, `Max relative difference among violations: 5.66160124e-07`.
  - At the commit: `2 passed`.
- **Fingerprint:** `8267e9c234437263`, from `2ca10868bed8a68e`.
- **Proposed changelog line:** "The test of whether the Gaussian process predicts well divided the errors of its predictions by the standard deviation of the latent function instead of that of an observation, and replaced deviations below 1e-8 by 1e-6. This gave outliers and early refits in deterministic runs. It now uses the standard deviation of an observation, the GP's noise included, as MATLAB BADS does."
- **Uncertain: gpyreg's noise factor.** `add_noise=True` includes the posterior's `sn2_mult`, the factor by which gpyreg 1.3.3 raises the noise when the factorization fails (W1-25, kept). MATLAB never raises it.
  - I chose the SD of the observation under the posterior that actually predicts, because its latent variance was computed with the raised noise.
  - This is not rare. In the fingerprint's three deterministic runs, 44, 28 and 49 of 74 stored statistics came from posteriors with `sn2_mult` of 10, 100 or 1000. The noisy runs had 0 of 37.
  - The alternative, the hyperparameter's noise alone, is one line at each call site: `np.sqrt(fs[index_acq]**2 + np.exp(2*gp.get_hyperparameters()[0]["noise_log_scale"].item()))`, and the test would drop `sn2_mult`. It would give smaller SDs at level 0.
  - This choice affects what the population comparison measures.
- **Uncertain: size at level 2.** In the level-2 test run, the base noise SD is about 1e-3 and the latent SD at least 0.94. The change there is at most 5.7e-7 relative. It matters mostly at levels 0 and 1.
