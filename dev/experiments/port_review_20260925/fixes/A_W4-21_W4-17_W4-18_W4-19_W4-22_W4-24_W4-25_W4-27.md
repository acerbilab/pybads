  - **At the parent:** `11 failed, 6 passed` (DID NOT RAISE). **At the commit:** 17 passed.
- **Fingerprint:** `360971bf1f0ba6cb`.
- **Changelog, proposed:**
  - Changed: "**`hedge_gamma`.** `BADS` raises `ValueError` for a `hedge_gamma` that is not a number from 0 to 1/n, n the number of searches in `search_method` (1/2 at default). 1.1.0, like MATLAB BADS, ran with any value: above 1/n the search hedge favored the search of lower gain, and above 1/(n − 1) it gave some searches a negative probability, so that they were never chosen."
  - Upgrading: "`BADS` raises `ValueError` for a `hedge_gamma` outside [0, 1/n], n the number of searches in `search_method` (1/2 at default)."
- **`hedge_beta` and `hedge_decay`** (not refused in this commit; runs of 150 evaluations, script `hedge_beta_decay.py`/`.out`). Every run completed without an exception, so each failure below is silent.
  - `hedge_beta`:
    - 0 gives a uniform softmax and works.
    - A negative value inverts the hedge, favoring the lower gain.
    - At -1000, `exp` overflows, and `inf` or `NaN` give NaN through `inf * 0`. The probabilities are NaN in all 41 searches, and so are the gains (`phat = NaN`), so every choice is the uniform fallback.
    - Safe range: finite and ≥ 0.
  - `hedge_decay`:
    - [0, 1] is sane; 1 means no decay, as in MATLAB.
    - Above 1, the gains grow geometrically: `max |g|` reached 2.4e13 at 2 and 9e68 at 50 in 41 searches. The choice locks onto one search down to `hedge_gamma`'s floor (ES-wcm chosen 38 of 41 times at 50). After about ln(1e308/10)/ln(decay) updates (~1020 at 2, ~180 at 50) the gains overflow, `inf - inf` gives NaN, and every later choice is random.
    - A negative value makes the gains alternate in sign: finite, but meaningless.
    - NaN makes the gains NaN from the first update, and every choice random.
