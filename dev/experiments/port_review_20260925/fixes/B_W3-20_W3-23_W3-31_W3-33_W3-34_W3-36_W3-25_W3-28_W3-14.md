- **Changelog (Fixed):** "**Start point on the grid.** A starting point halfway between two points of the search grid goes to the one farther from zero, as in MATLAB BADS; 1.1.0 took the even one: `x0 = [1, 3]` in a plausible box `[-2048, 2048]` starts at `[2, 4]`, not `[0, 4]`."
- **Upgrading from:** covered by "Results differ from 1.1.0".
