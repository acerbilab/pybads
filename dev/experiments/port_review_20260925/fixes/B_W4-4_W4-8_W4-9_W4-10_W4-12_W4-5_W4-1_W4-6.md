## 2. W4-8: `c693c47` fix: the function logger refuses a malformed SD with its documented ValueError, and checks both outputs before recording anything (W4-8)

**Files:**
- `pybads/function_logger/function_logger.py`
- `pybads/testing/function_logger/test_function_logger.py`

**What changed:**
- The logger's own conversion (`.item()`) has moved out of the `try` around the target, so its errors no longer carry the note "Error in executing the logged function".
- The value and, at level 2, the SD are converted the same way by a new private helper `_as_scalar`: an array or list of one element is taken as that element (`.item()`).
- Both are checked the same way by a second private helper, `_is_finite_real_scalar`: the value must be a scalar of kind `b`/`i`/`u`/`f` and finite; the SD must also be positive.
- Both checks run before `_record`, so a refusal leaves the log unchanged.
- A complex type is refused whatever its imaginary part, as MATLAB's `isreal` refuses it (`funlogger.m:103-110`).
- `True` is still accepted as a value, as in MATLAB and at 1.1.0.

**Tests:**

  - the malformed outputs of the verifier's `v7_misc.py`, plus a complex SD;
  - each must raise the documented message (`InvalidFuncValue`, `InvalidNoiseValue`, or the `specify_target_noise` format message) without the `FuncError` note;
  - after one valid call, `Xn`, `X_max_idx`, `func_count`, `X` and `Y` must be unchanged.
- `test_call_one_element_sd_taken_as_a_number`: an SD of `np.array([0.5])`, `[0.5]` or `np.array([[0.5]])` is taken as 0.5.
- At the parent, 10 of the 20 new cases fail:
  - TypeErrors: `float() argument must be … not 'complex'`, `ufunc 'isfinite' not supported`, `'<=' not supported between … 'list'`;
  - NumPy's messages, including the one with the `FuncError` note;
  - `DID NOT RAISE` for `np.complex128(1+0j)`.
- At the commit: `46 passed`.

**Fingerprint:** `360971bf1f0ba6cb`, unchanged.

**Changelog (Fixed):** "**Malformed target outputs.** A target value, or with `specify_target_noise=True` a noise SD, that is not a finite real scalar (and, for the SD, positive) raises the documented `ValueError` before anything is recorded. 1.1.0 raised `TypeError` or NumPy's own errors for some of them (an SD of `None`, a list or a string, a complex value), blamed the target for an array of more than one element, and accepted a NumPy complex value. An SD given as a one-element list is now taken as its number, as the value already was."

## 7. W4-1: `e74e12a` fix: the initial design is seeded from the run's generator, so that random_seed decides it (W4-1)

**Files:**
- `pybads/init_functions/init_sobol.py`
- `AGENTS.md`
- `pybads/testing/init_functions/test_init_sobol.py`
- `pybads/testing/bads/test_bads_seed.py`

**What changed:**
- The seed derived from `u0` is removed, with its `uint64` cast (and with it W4-2's platform dependence), along with the unreachable non-finite branch. The seed is now `seed = get_rng(rng).integers(2**63)`, passed to `Sobol(D, seed=seed)` as before.
- **Why one draw rather than passing the generator:** the run's generator then advances by exactly one draw at the design, whatever scipy's `Sobol` draws for its scrambling. That number depends on D and on scipy's implementation, so passing the generator would let a scipy change shift every later draw of the run. Keeping `seed=` with an integer also stays compatible with SciPy 1.13, whose `Sobol` has no `rng` keyword.
- **Why the range `[0, 2**63)` and not the old `[1, 997]`:** 997 is MATLAB's bound on its skip index (`MaxSeed`), which means nothing for a scrambling seed and would allow only 997 designs per D.
- The docstring says the scrambling is seeded by one draw of `rng` and that only `u0`'s size is read. A comment cites `initSobol.m:9-15` as the departure from MATLAB.
- `AGENTS.md`'s sentence on the Sobol seed now reads: "The scrambling of the Sobol design is seeded by one draw of the generator in `init_sobol`, so that the seed decides the design, whatever the start; MATLAB BADS's design depends on the start alone."

**Tests:**
- `test_init_sobol.py::test_init_sobol_design_follows_the_generator_not_the_start`, at D = 3: four starts (interior ones, and ones on the ±1 bounds) give one design for one generator seed, and seeds 0 and 1 give two designs.
- `test_bads_seed.py::test_seed_decides_initial_design`: the log's design rows in runs of 10 evaluations. Starts at `x0 = 4`, at (-5, 0, 10) and on the plausible lower bound give one design at `random_seed` 42; seed 43 gives another.
- Both fail at the parent: `assert np.array_equal(...)` is False for the start on the bound.
- A separate check at the parent, for the seed part:
  - `BADS: seeds 42 and 43 give the same design: True`
  - `init_sobol: rng 0 and 1 give the same design: True`
- At the commit, both are False. `test_bads_seed.py` and `test_init_sobol.py` give 26 passed.

**Fingerprint:** `663b49edc9320c55`.

**Changelog (Fixed):** "**Initial design.** The scrambling of the initial Sobol design is seeded from the run's generator, so that `random_seed` decides the design, as it decides every other random draw of a run; MATLAB BADS's design depends on the start alone. In 1.1.0 the design depended on neither the seed nor the start: every start inside the plausible box gave one design for each number of variables, and a start on or below a plausible lower bound gave a design that could differ between x86 and Apple silicon. Results change at default options."
- **Upgrading from:** covered by the existing "Results differ from 1.1.0, also with a fixed seed."

**Uncertain:**
- The seeded tests of `test_bads_optimization.py` and the other suite files were not run. The orchestrator's fast suite and `tolerance_sweep.py` apply.
