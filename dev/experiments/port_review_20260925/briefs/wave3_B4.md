## How PyBADS reaches this code at default options

Every run: each iteration ends in a poll, unless a successful search of the round skips it (`skip_poll_after_search`). The poll draws 2D directions from `poll_mads_2n`: a random lower-triangular basis whose integer entries are bounded by `max(1, round(search_mesh_size / mesh_size))`, its rows and columns permuted, divided by the GP's `poll_scale`, together with its negation. It multiplies them by the mesh size and by `poll_scale`, adds them to the incumbent, and drops the points outside the bounds, already evaluated or violating `non_box_cons` (`contraints_check`; the points are not put on the grid, `force_poll_mesh` being off). It rebuilds the local GP at its first step and whenever a refit, a move or a failed rebuild asks for it (slice B5's), predicts the target at the best point polled so far, ranks the remaining poll points by LCB and evaluates the best one, until the set is empty, the budget is spent, 2D points are evaluated, or `_is_poll_stop_` stops it (`complete_poll` off): after a good poll, when the probability that none of the best remaining points improves on the target exceeds `1 - tol_poi`, or when the GP is unreliable; without a good poll, only after `min_failed_poll_steps` evaluations, which is infinite by default. A poll that improves on the incumbent by more than the sufficient improvement moves the incumbent (with `sloppy_improvement`, on by default, any improvement does) and expands the mesh (`mesh_size_integer` plus one, capped at `max_poll_grid_number`); otherwise the mesh shrinks, twice when the improvement over the last `accelerate_mesh_steps` iterations is below `tol_fun` (`accelerate_mesh`, on), and the search mesh follows. The target is predicted from the GP under the hyperparameters of the best iteration at every uncertainty level, since `uncertain_incumbent` is on by default, and set below the prediction by `sd_level`. At uncertainty level 1 or 2 the incumbent's value is a GP estimate, each poll point's value is the GP's prediction after the point is added, and the improvement takes `improvement_quantile`. Say for every finding whether a default run reaches it, at which uncertainty level (0 deterministic, 1 noise inferred, 2 `specify_target_noise`), and which option or input reaches it otherwise.
