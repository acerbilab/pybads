# gpyreg 1.3.3 for PyBADS

What gpyreg 1.3.3 changes in PyBADS, measured on 2026-09-24 and 25 before
PyBADS moved its minimum gpyreg and its CI pin from 1.3.1 to 1.3.3
(`c588686`; tooling plan `dev/plans/tooling-and-rng.md`, Phase 9). The
gpyreg maintainers, preparing 1.3.2 and 1.3.3, asked PyBADS for the
effect of the release and for anything in it that should change before
the tag; this note answers both.

**Verdict.** The test suite passes, no invalid-input refusal of gpyreg
fires, and at default options 1.3.3 gives PyBADS the results of 1.3.2.
The only change is that of 1.3.2's predictions for a GP with very small
noise, a regime BADS's GP reaches on many deterministic targets: the
benchmark comparison flags five configurations, each with runs ending
closer to the minimum, one unflagged configuration ends slightly farther
from it (`ackley_D6`, within its tolerance), and no run outside that
regime changes. Nothing in 1.3.3 needs to change for PyBADS.

gpyreg 1.3.2 forms the predictive covariance of a GP whose smallest noise
variance is below `1e-6` from a Cholesky factor, and refuses several
invalid inputs with `ValueError`; 1.3.3 (`98ab5a4`) makes a `fit`,
`update` or `set_hyperparameters` that raises restore the GP as it was
before the call, and gives the result of 1.3.2 for every call that
completes (gpyreg's `docsrc/source/release_notes.rst`). PyBADS's GPs reach
the low-noise regime at default options: the lower bound of the noise
hyperparameter, `log(tol_fun) - 1`, is a variance of about `1.35e-7`.

## Setup

- PyBADS at `be11a52` (suite, 1.3.2–1.3.3 check) and `2059506`
  (population, issue checks); the commits between them change only
  records. Every random draw goes through the run's generator `bads.rng`.
- gpyreg from clones at the tags, selected with `PYTHONPATH`: v1.3.1
  `1dbbfc5`, v1.3.2 `29b868c`, v1.3.3 `98ab5a4`; `gpyreg.__file__` printed
  before each run. The earlier issue checks cited for comparison (1.3.1
  and 1.3.2, 2026-09-24) ran from the editable checkout of 1.3.1 and from
  the worktree of the 1.3.2 branch before its tag (`755a4b3`, whose
  package code differs from `v1.3.2` in a docstring only).
- Windows 11, Python 3.12.6, NumPy 2.5.3, SciPy 1.18.1, the project venv.

## Test suite

`pytest -p no:rerunfailures -q -rfE`, three runs under each gpyreg:

| gpyreg | runs | outcome per run |
|---|---|---|
| 1.3.1 | 3 | 109 passed |
| 1.3.3 | 3 | 109 passed |

No test fails, no `ValueError` of a refusal is raised, and the warnings
are of the same kinds under both.

## 1.3.3 against 1.3.2

- `dev/scripts/fingerprint.py` prints `57241c985a68c78b` under both
  (1.3.1: `91ca34c6b51e7f20`).
- Six configurations of the `default` suite in which every counted run had
  failed fits in `_robust_gp_fit_` in the 1.3.1 counts of 2026-09-24
  (`sphere_D2`, `ellipsoid_D3`, `rosenbrock_D2`, `ellipsoid_D3_homo`,
  `sphere_nonbox_D3`, `ellipsoid_D3_unbounded`; the seventh such
  configuration, `ellipsoid_D6`, was not included), seeds 0–9: the 60
  records are identical in every field of the result but the wall time.

1.3.2 and 1.3.3 leave a GP whose fit failed in different states, and the
retry in `_robust_gp_fit_` goes on with that GP. At default options it
reads from it only bounds that PyBADS sets itself (`_gp_hyp`), so the
retry, and the run, does not depend on the difference; with
`use_slice_sampler=True` or the `negquad` mean it does (survey, candidate
table). At default options the population below therefore stands for
1.3.2 as well.

## The two PyBADS-side issues

`dev/scripts/gpyreg_issue_checks.py` under 1.3.3 at `2059506`:

- **Known-noise path.** With `fit_lik=False`, the run stops at its first
  GP setup with `ValueError: Unknown hyperprior type delta`, raised in
  gpyreg's `_write_prior_block` from `set_priors`, as under 1.3.2 (under
  1.3.1 from `set_priors` itself). `_gp_hyp` sets the noise prior
  `("delta", noise_mu)`, which no gpyreg release implements.
- **Failed fits in `_robust_gp_fit_`.** Over the 18 configurations, seeds
  0–9 (180 runs), 3,385 calls: 2,865 with no failed fit, 307 with one,
  201 with two, 12 with three. No call reached the fifth consecutive
  failure, at which the raised lower bound of the noise passes its upper
  bound and the call raises; every run finished. Under 1.3.1 and 1.3.2
  (15 configurations, draws through NumPy's global stream) the most was
  also three.

## Follow-ups

- Done: `gpyreg >= 1.3.3` and `GPYREG_PIN` at `98ab5a4` (`c588686`); the
  1.3.3 population is the reference for later comparisons; the fingerprint
  under the v1.3.3 clone is `57241c985a68c78b`.
- For PyBADS, recorded in the survey and `dev/TODO.md`, none of them a
  gpyreg matter: the `"delta"` noise prior of the known-noise path; the
  noise bound of `_robust_gp_fit_`, which inverts at the fifth consecutive
  failure (not reached at default options); passing the retry's data to
  `_robust_gp_fit_`'s GP explicitly, so that it does not depend on what a
  failed fit leaves; and the three unguarded GP calls behind the crashes.
