# The GP layer's numerical health at `a4dcd65`

Counts of what the GP layer does in PyBADS runs at default options,
measured on 2026-09-28 on `dev-next` after the port review's four waves
(`a4dcd65`, #81), with gpyreg 1.3.3, for four open items of `dev/TODO.md`:
gpyreg's inflation of the GP noise (W1-25), the zero predictive SDs at
uncertainty level 0, the NaN log prior of the GP mean (a point of the
`ellipsoid_D3_hetero` item) and the GP on a one-point training set. The
tables, the command and the provenance are in
[`experiments/gp_health_linux_20260928/`](../experiments/gp_health_linux_20260928/README.md).

**Verdict.** The factorization of the training covariance fails routinely
on smooth targets with a large range, and the zero SDs are the same
condition seen from the predictions: both come from a GP whose signal
variance exceeds its noise variance by 1e12 to 1e20, which the bounds of
the output scale and of the noise admit, in PyBADS as in MATLAB BADS. On
those targets gpyreg rescues most factorizations by inflating the noise, the
inflated noise stays in the posteriors that the search and the poll use,
and the refits that fail cost up to 42% of a run's time. The NaN log prior
of the mean no longer occurs (W1-23 removed it), and training sets of one
or two points occur only under non-box constraints.

## Setup

PyBADS at `a4dcd65`'s package code, gpyreg 1.3.3 from a clone at its tag,
Linux, Python 3.11.15, NumPy 2.4.6, SciPy 1.17.1, one BLAS thread per run:
the environment of the Linux reference `population_linux_wave4_20260927`,
whose fingerprint the checkout printed. The suites `default`, `geometry`,
`oned` and `bounds` of `dev/scripts/benchmark_targets.py`, seeds 0-29,
1,080 runs. The counters (`dev/scripts/gp_health_hooks/sitecustomize.py`)
wrap gpyreg's `GP` in every run through `PYTHONPATH` and observe only: the
default suite's 540 records equal the reference's but for the wall time.
The tables are `dev/scripts/gp_health.py`'s.

## Failed factorizations and the inflated noise (W1-25)

gpyreg 1.3.3 factors `K + sn2_mult * sn2 * I`, multiplies `sn2_mult` by ten
after each failed attempt, and raises after ten; the posterior keeps the
multiplier, which the hyperparameters do not show. MATLAB BADS raises at
the first failure (`CholAttempts = 0`, `bads.m:272`;
`infExact_fastrobust.m:80`), and `gpHyperOptimize.m` restarts the fit with
a nudged noise, as PyBADS's `_robust_gp_fit_` does after gpyreg raises.

At level 0, over 780 runs, 20.5% of the evaluations of the fit's objective
needed an inflated noise and 15.6% of the 9,790 refits of the local GP
needed a second try; 51 refits failed all ten tries and kept the best of
their starts. The failed tries take 9.2% of the runs' wall time. The rates
depend on the target, not on the dimension or the level:

- heavy on smooth targets with a large range of values: `ellipsoid_D3`
  (50% of the objective's factorizations inflated, 64% of refits retried,
  6.5% failed outright, 42% of the run's time in failed tries), its
  unbounded twin, `ellipsoid_D6` (38%, 35%, 22% of the time),
  `rosenbrock_D2`, `sphere_D2`, `sphere_nonbox_D3`, the spheres of the
  `bounds` suite, `sphere_band_D3`; lighter on `ellipsoid_D10`,
  `sphere_D10`, the 1-D spheres and ellipsoid, `edgesphere_D2`;
- none or almost none on the rugged and the real-data targets
  (`ackley_*`, `rastrigin_*`, `ridge_*`, `timing_D5`,
  `multisensory_s1_D6`), on `rosenbrock_D6` and on the log-scaled spheres;
- with noise, on the ellipsoids alone: `ellipsoid_D3_homo` (31% of the
  objective, 55% of refits retried) and `ellipsoid_D3_hetero` (31%, never
  ten failures in a row); the noisy spheres and `multisensory_s1_D6_homo`
  none.

Which retries leave a worse GP: on the affected targets 15% to 58% of the
fits that return, and 18% to 67% of the updates, hand on a posterior whose
noise is multiplied, by up to 1e6 (the median over the runs of
`ellipsoid_D3` of the largest multiplier); 21% to 68% of the poll's
predictions on those targets come from such a posterior. On `ellipsoid_D3`
the runs whose poll predictions more often come from an inflated posterior
end farther from the minimum (Spearman's rho +0.72 over the 30 seeds, p <
0.001), a correlation that does not show which causes which; on
`ellipsoid_D3_hetero` there is none (rho 0.00), although 58% of its fits
keep a noise multiplied by ten or more, on an output variance of about
2e16 against a noise variance of about 11.

The cause is the ratio of the two variances. The output scale is bounded
by `[log(tol_fun), log(1e6 * tol_fun / tol_mesh)]`, an output variance of
up to 1e18 at the defaults, and the noise by `log(tol_fun) - 1`, a variance
of at least 1.35e-7 (`_gp_hyp`, as `gpdefBads.m:96` and `161`): the bounds
admit a ratio of about 1e25, far past double precision, where a Cholesky
factorization fails from a ratio of about 1e16. The posteriors kept
inflated sit at ratios of 1e15 to 1e18. MATLAB BADS has the same bounds,
so its fits meet the
same matrices; it stops at the first failure where gpyreg goes on.

## Zero predictive SDs

The latent variance that `predict` returns is exactly 0 at 9.9% of the
poll's acquisition calls at level 0 (in 38% of `sphere_D2`'s and 37% of
`sphere_nonbox_D3`'s, 40% of `sphere_D3_nopb`'s), 5.0% at level 1 (34% of
`ellipsoid_D3_homo`'s) and 7.4% at level 2 (18% of
`ellipsoid_D3_hetero`'s), and at similar shares of the search's candidates
and of the target's predictions. The targets are those of the failed
factorizations, the spheres of the `geometry` suite added. A zero SD at a
poll candidate makes `gamma_z` infinite and the GP unreliable for that
poll (W3-28): after a good poll the poll stops, and without one it cannot
be skipped.

The zeros are rounding. Recomputed from the first posterior as `predict`
computes it, the variance before gpyreg's clamp at 0 is negative at 91% of
the level-0 zeros (98% at level 1, 94% at level 2) and exactly 0 at the
rest, and its size is below 1e-13 of the prior variance `kss` at 96% of
them (99% with noise): the difference `kss - v'v` has cancelled to the
rounding of `kss`. All but 0.02% of the points lie within 0.1 length
scales of a training input (74% within 1e-3), and their prior variance
exceeds the effective noise by 1e12 to 1e20: near the data the true
variance is of the order of the noise, below the rounding of the signal.
At level 0, 34% to 100% of them, by target, are in gpyreg's low-noise
representation (noise below 1e-6), whose prediction carries the rounding
of the inverse; with noise, almost none. MATLAB's `mygp.m:187` clamps the
same way (`fs2 = max(fs2, 0)`), so with the same hyperparameters it gives
the same zeros; how often MATLAB's own fits reach them only MATLAB can
show.

## The log prior and the priors outside their bounds

No log prior and no objective was NaN or infinite in the 1,080 runs, and no
fit had the prior of the GP mean outside the mean's bounds: W1-23
(`172df00`, wave 1 of the port review), which made the mean unbounded as
in MATLAB, removed the route of the Windows reference at `ab4dded`
([`population_gpfixes_20260925`](../experiments/population_gpfixes_20260925/README.md),
67 runs). The priors of the noise (up to 19% of the fits, `ridge_D2`) and
of the output scale (up to 12%, `sphere_D10`) do fall outside their bounds
at small meshes, by at most 2.1 and 4.8 standard deviations in the
recorded examples, with no zero normalization constant: the noise prior's
centre moves with `mesh_noise_multiplier * log(mesh_size)`, as MATLAB's
(`gpdefBads.m:207`), below the same bound.

## One- and two-point training sets

Only the non-box constraints reach them, which leave few feasible points
of the initial design. Every run of `sphere_band_D2` fits its GP once, on
`x0` alone, and ends after 2 evaluations (W2-37); every run of
`sphere_band_D3` holds a one-point training set at four calls of the GP on
average (fits, updates, hyperparameter settings) and then converges, in 45
to 62 evaluations. Two runs of `sphere_nonbox_D3` (seeds 22 and 29) hold
two distinct points at three calls each, W3-40's case, and finish.

## For `dev/TODO.md` (after the Close step)

- **W1-25.** The counts the item asks for are above. gpyreg's switch
  `raise_on_cholesky_failure` turns every inflated evaluation into a
  failed try, a fifth of the level-0 objective and half of it on
  `ellipsoid_D3`, which is consistent with wave 1's measurement that the
  switch made the ellipsoids worse. The alternative the item names, a
  jitter scaled to the signal and shared by the fit and the predictions,
  bounds the ratio that causes both the failures and the zero SDs; it is a
  departure from MATLAB. Either needs its population against the Linux
  reference, with the `geometry` and `bounds` suites, which hold affected
  configurations; the counters measure what it changes.
- **Zero SDs.** Counted and traced: the same condition as W1-25's, seen
  from the predictions, and the same clamp as MATLAB's. The item can join
  W1-25's; its comparison with MATLAB's frequency stays for a developer
  with MATLAB.
- **`ellipsoid_D3_hetero`.** The NaN log prior of its third point no
  longer occurs. New for the item: its GP runs at a variance ratio near
  1e15, and most of its fits keep a noise multiplied by ten or more.
- **The one-point GP.** Reached only under non-box constraints: on one
  point in every run of the thin bands, on two in 2 of the 30 runs of
  `sphere_nonbox_D3`; the runs at D = 3 go on to converge.
- **Time.** Failed refits cost 9% of the level-0 run time over the suites
  and up to 42% on one configuration: a measure for the profiler item and
  for any change to the refits.
